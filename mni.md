9. MY METHOD
Eyebrow:
How I work
A clear process. No unnecessary theatre.
01 · Listen
Tell me what is happening.
Ordinary language is enough.
No formal brief.
No complicated specification.
No need to diagnose the problem yourself.
________________________________________
02 · Understand
Find out what is really causing difficulty.
Who is affected?
What happens today?
Where does the friction appear?
What information is involved?
What does a better result actually mean?
________________________________________
03 · Simplify
Find the clearest useful direction.
Before adding anything new, look for what can be removed, clarified, reorganised or made easier.
________________________________________
04 · Shape
Design the solution around the real need.
The method should follow the problem.
The problem should never be forced into a favourite method.
________________________________________
05 · Deliver
Create what people can actually use.
A useful solution should fit the real environment, the people involved and the outcome that matters.
________________________________________
06 · Check
Return to the original problem.
Did the work actually improve what needed to improve?
If not, keep learning.
________________________________________
07 · Improve
Stay useful as reality changes.
People learn.
Businesses grow.
Needs evolve.
A responsible solution should improve when evidence shows that improvement is needed.
